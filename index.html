State Memorial Announcement

The Passing of Felix Dzherzinsky 🕊️ Official address delivered by General Secretary Kewl Menzinsky

To all citizens of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics,

Today, we gather under circumstances that none of us had wished to witness. The office of the Secretariat of the Central Committee formally announces the passing of General Secretary Felix, whose name has become part of the history of this server.

When the U.S.S.R. was rebuilt and turned into what it is today, only a few could have imagined the heights it would ultimately reach. We started as a small server which was deeply inactive, with no certainty that we would last. Today we have passed 7,000 members. That did not happen overnight, and it certainly did not happen because of one lucky moment. It happened because people kept showing up and putting in the work. Felix was one of those people from the very beginning. He was among those who did not fall for temporary emotions and prioritised the server first. At that time, none carried a greater burden than Felix. When everyone left, the sole person standing was Felix.

His leadership was not about roles, authority, or vague things like power, but about the countless hours devoted to keeping the server together after a traumatic period left to him by Leon, strengthening its institutions through the many decrees he made, and inspiring a generation of brothers like myself, Secretary Spoop, Secretary Speer Menzhinsky, and most of the current senior members of the Central Committee, who would continue his work and propel his ideology forward.

Whether one agreed with every decision he made or not, no honest person can deny that he was a very important and instrumental leader and, most importantly, a genuine friend who gave us all a vision aligned with morality and Islam. He set an example for how a moral state ought to be run. Felix was not a perfect man, and he never claimed to be. He made decisions that people agreed with and decisions that people criticised. That comes with leading anything for nearly seven years. But judging his entire legacy by a few moments would be deeply unfair. Such is the nature of mankind. Justice demands that we weigh almost seven years of service with genuine fairness and not reduce a person's entire legacy to isolated moments beyond their control. General Secretary Felix devoted himself to this server for no less than seven years, and for that he deserves our gratitude and our remembrance.

One aspect of Felix that many people never truly came to understand was the place that Islam occupied within his heart. To many outside observers, he was known first as the Generalissimus, an administrator, or an ideologue. To me, however, having had the privilege of serving as his only Secretariat member and his second-in-command for years, and speaking with him privately on countless occasions, there was always something much deeper. Behind the public figure was a man whose greatest source of conviction was his faith in Allah SWT.

Our private conversations rarely remained confined to server politics, administration, or anything of that sort. More often than not, they would turn towards religion, philosophy, morality, history, the purpose of life, and what we as Muslims ought to do. He spoke about Islam not merely as an identity, but as a complete way of life. He believed that no ideology, political system, or worldly success could ever compare to the guidance revealed by Allah سبحانه وتعالى. He genuinely wanted people to find Islam. Whether they agreed with him politically did not matter nearly as much as whether they found what he believed was the truth. He believed that the greatest gift one person could offer another was sincere guidance towards Islam. Everything else came second.

“Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in a way that is best.” — Qur'an 16:125

There were many occasions where he would speak to me at length about individuals who openly opposed him, criticised him, spoke badly about him behind his back, or even despised him. Surprisingly, his concern was rarely about himself. Instead, he would tell me that he genuinely hoped those same people would one day find peace, become better individuals, and, above all else, be guided towards Islam. Despite everything said against him, he often expressed that he held no desire to see those who wished him harm ruined. He wanted what he believed was best for them.

This is perhaps the side of Felix that the public rarely saw. Most judged only the public figure. I came to know the man himself. Behind every decision was someone who constantly reflected upon faith, repentance, mercy, and the hope that even those who opposed him might one day become his brothers in Islam.

On a much more personal note, the truth is that I would not be here today if it were not for Felix. Everything I have become, every responsibility I have carried, every opportunity I have been given, and every honour I now hold, I owe to Felix. Out of everyone, he chose me. He entrusted me to be his sole adviser, his second-in-command, and the only person upon whom he relied for the day-to-day administration of this server.

Ironically, our relationship did not begin as one of friendship. When I was merely a member of the Political Bureau of the Central Committee, I argued with him constantly. I questioned his decisions, hated his judgement, and believed that I could do better. Looking back now, I realise how little I truly knew. Leadership is easy when everything is going well. Felix led when everything seemed to be falling apart.

Today, I can say with complete sincerity that he was the right man for his time. I genuinely do not believe that anyone else could have carried this server through those years as he did. If he had never promoted me to serve in his Secretariat, I would not hold this rank today.

May Allah ﷻ reward him abundantly for every good he brought into this community, forgive his shortcomings, and make life easier for him.

Thank you for everything, Felix Dzherzinsky.

In the coming days, the Central Committee of the MSPSU will organise a State Funeral in honour of the late General Secretary Felix, followed by an Official Inauguration and Victory Parade to mark the beginning of a new chapter in the history of the server. Further details regarding these State ceremonies will be released shortly.
