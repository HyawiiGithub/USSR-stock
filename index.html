Quick Reference

The National Money Cycle

Rubles spent on State fees and approved store items return to the treasury. Weekly national budgets then fund sectors according to activity, productivity, approved contracts, and decisions of the Congress. Sector spending and attachment maintenance return money to the treasury again.
